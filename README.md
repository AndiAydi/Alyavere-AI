# Alyavere-AI

### Adaptive Learning Companion for Students

**Alyavere AI** is an AI-powered learning companion designed to help students learn more effectively through personalized explanations, adaptive practice, and progress evaluation.

This project was developed as part of a *Karya Ilmiah Remaja (KIR)* research at **SMA Negeri 1 Mertoyudan**.

---

## Background

Every student has a different learning style and pace. Traditional classroom teaching often uses the same approach for all students, which can make some students struggle to understand the material or lose motivation.

Alyavere AI aims to solve this problem by acting as a personal learning companion that adapts to each student’s needs.

---

## Key Features

- **Initial Assessment**  
  Measures the student’s current understanding before starting a lesson.

- **Adaptive Explanation**  
  Explains materials in different ways based on the student’s level of understanding.

- **Adaptive Practice Questions**  
  Provides exercises that adjust in difficulty according to the student’s performance.

- **Learning Evaluation**  
  Reviews the student’s progress and identifies topics that need more attention.

---

## Research Objectives

- Develop a prototype of an AI Companion that can adapt its teaching style to the student.
- Measure the effectiveness of the AI Companion on student learning outcomes.
- Help students who struggle to keep up with regular classroom learning.
